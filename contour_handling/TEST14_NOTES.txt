CVI contours TEST14
===================

Interactive inversion-recovery polarity editing
------------------------------------------------
TEST13 exposed an interactive diagnostic but incorrectly assumed the advanced
T1 fitter's result fields (tSeconds/displaySignal). The normal ROI/CVI workflow
uses ffc_simple_t1dispersion, whose fields are timesMs/signedSignal. TEST14 fixes
the diagnostic for that real data structure.

The simple IR fit has also been corrected at source:

- Automatic sign selection is now independent for each evolution field.
- Each field therefore has its own automatic early-negative prefix length.
- Clicking a point in Check fits flips that individual point's sign and refits.
- Manual negative/positive indices are stored on the ImageReconCore scan object.
- Manual choices are honoured by subsequent simple-IR refits until Reset manual
  signs is pressed.
- The diagnostic shows square markers for manually overridden points.
- The overview reports the automatic negative-prefix count for each field.

This change applies to ROI and CVI-mask T1 dispersion through
ffc_simple_t1dispersion. The voxel-wise ffc_simple_t1map path is intentionally
unchanged and still uses its existing study-wide data-selected prefix.
