CVI contours TEST13
===================

Interactive inversion-recovery polarity editing
------------------------------------------------
When "Check fits" is enabled for an ROI or CVI-mask T1 dispersion analysis,
the app now opens an interactive fit diagnostic rather than the legacy
read-only dashboard.

- Click any recovery data point to force the opposite signed polarity.
- The fit is recalculated immediately for all evolution fields.
- Manually constrained points are drawn as square markers.
- "Reset manual signs" removes all user polarity constraints and returns to
  automatic polarity selection.
- The physical IR model remains a single monotonic recovery with at most one
  zero crossing. A click that cannot be reconciled with that model is rejected.
- Manual negative/positive sample constraints are stored on the scan object and
  survive subsequent refits until reset.

The modern ROI T1dispersion fit does not use one fixed number of negative
samples for every field. It estimates the recovery polarity independently per
field. The older simple voxel-wise T1 map implementation still uses a common,
data-selected negative prefix; that mapping path is unchanged in TEST13.
