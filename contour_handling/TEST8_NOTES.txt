CVI contours TEST 8
===================
Adds an explicit handedness/reflection correction to TEST 7.

Why:
A mirror reflection cannot be corrected with the six rigid registration
parameters. The TEST 7 screenshot shows the LV scale/tilt are plausible but
the RV lies on the opposite side of the LV, consistent with a left/right
reflection.

Changes:
- Registration dialog has Mirror left/right and Mirror up/down checkboxes.
- Reflection axes are frozen to the FCI image orientation when
  "Use current FCI scan as target" is pressed.
- Reflection occurs about the clinical contour-stack centre before the
  proper rigid-body rotations, so it does not translate the LV across the FOV.
- Reset registration clears both mirror flags.

For the supplied SAX test, try Mirror left/right first with all rotations at 0.
