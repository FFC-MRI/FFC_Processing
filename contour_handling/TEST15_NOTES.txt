CVI contours TEST15
===================

T1-dispersion inversion-recovery fitting now uses a physically plausible
T1 range of 20--1500 ms when choosing the signed-magnitude zero crossing.

For each evolution field independently, every allowed early-negative /
late-positive sign transition is fitted. Candidates whose fitted T1 falls
outside 20--1500 ms are rejected from automatic selection. The lowest-SSE
candidate inside the range is used.

Manual point choices in the Check Fits diagnostic are hard sign constraints.
After a click, the unconstrained points are re-selected automatically and the
sign transition is moved as needed to retain a 20--1500 ms T1 where possible.
If no sign pattern consistent with the manual constraints gives a plausible
T1, that field is excluded from the dispersion curve (T1/R1 = NaN) and the
best unconstrained fit is shown dashed for diagnosis.

The fit result stores:
  results.plausibleT1RangeMs
  results.signCandidateT1ms
  results.signCandidatePlausible
and per field:
  rawT1ms / rawR1
  fitWithinPlausibleRange
  candidateT1ms
  plausibleCandidateMask

The voxel-wise ffc_simple_t1map path is unchanged in this build.
